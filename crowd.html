Ericeira Crowd Index

How busy are Ericeira's surf spots? Every two hours, from sunrise to sunset, Erisurf counts the surfers in the water on the live webcams at the most popular breaks: Ribeira d'Ilhas, the World Surfing Reserve's best-known right-hand point; Pedra Branca, the reef just north of town; Praia do Norte and Praia do Sul in town; and Foz do Lizandro, the beach break at the river mouth where the surf schools go. The charts show which spots fill up and at what time of day, so you can pick an emptier lineup, or come back when the crowd has gone.

Surfers counted on the cams since Mon 21 Sep. Each spot has its own scale, recomputed every run from its own counts: quiet up to the 25th percentile of non-empty lineups, normal to the median, busy to the 90th, packed above. Grey dots are empty lineups; the line is a smoothed average over about half a day. The vertical scale is a square root, so small lineups keep some room without dominating. Nights are cut out: each column is one day's daylight, morning on its left and dusk on its right, and the thin lines are the nights.
