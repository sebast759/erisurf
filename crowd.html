Crowd

Surfers counted on the cams since Mon 21 Sep. Each spot has its own scale, recomputed every run from its own counts: quiet up to the 25th percentile of non-empty lineups, normal to the median, busy to the 90th, packed above. Grey dots are empty lineups; the line is a smoothed average over about half a day. The vertical scale is a square root, so small lineups keep some room without dominating. Nights are cut out: each column is one day's daylight, morning on its left and dusk on its right, and the thin lines are the nights.
